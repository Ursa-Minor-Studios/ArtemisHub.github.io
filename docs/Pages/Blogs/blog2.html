Artemis III Booster Stacked and NASA shares Artemis Updates

NASA has officially finished stacking the SRB boosters and in the next few months will roll SLS out of the VAB for testing.

Hardware Updates

NASA is currently in the process of repairing the damaged on the flame trench caused from the Artemis II launch. Do to improvements since Artemis I, this damage has been minimized. Development on the Orion capsule is progressing which they have recently done inspection on the solar arrays.

Crew Updates

The astronauts continue to go through their training performing various activities. The crew recently sampled food to choose what they want to eat for the mission. They have recently went down to San Diego to participate in recovery training.

Last September the crew met down in Florida for the first time since their announcement. They have met with SpaceX and Blue Origin as well as visiting Orion.
